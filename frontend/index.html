Integer Paise Precision

Eliminates IEEE 754 float rounding errors by tracking every rupee down to integer paise (1 Rupee = 100 paise).

🛡️

3-Strike Brute Force Lockout

Passwords and 4-digit PINs are secured with salted bcrypt. 3 failed attempts lock the account and persist across server reboots.

⚡

Atomic Transfers & Rollbacks

Database-level ACID atomic transactions ensure sender debit and receiver credit share one transaction ID or rollback completely.

💳

ReportLab Document Engine
